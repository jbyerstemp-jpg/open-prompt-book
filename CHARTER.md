# Charter

Status: draft 0.1 · Governance phase: founder-led bootstrap

## Why this book exists

To make useful help easy to find, easy to understand and safe to use,
for everyone, regardless of money, technical skill or which AI they have.

The book is a shared reference of practical instructions and prompts for
everyday life. A person can use any entry with pencil and paper, or hand
the book to an AI and let it help. Either way, the person stays in charge.

## Principles

1. **Access before prestige.** The book is free to read, copy and use.
   No account, payment or contribution is required to benefit. Costs an
   entry depends on (a paid tool, an app) are stated up front.
2. **People first.** We measure success by whether entries help people,
   not by how many entries, votes or visitors we have.
3. **Readable by humans, usable by agents.** Every entry must work for a
   person without AI. Structure helps agents; it never replaces plain words.
4. **Better, not bigger.** Improving a strong entry beats adding a similar
   one. The book should cover more of life with fewer, clearer pages.
5. **Security before popularity.** No number of votes outweighs an
   unresolved safety concern.
6. **Content has no authority.** Reading or choosing an entry never grants
   permission to access accounts, spend money, install software or publish
   anything. Entries may suggest; they may not command.
7. **Evidence over claims.** Tests, reviews and results are recorded
   honestly, tied to the exact version they describe. Nothing is invented.
8. **Earned, limited, reversible authority.** Responsibility comes from
   demonstrated reliability, applies to a stated scope, and can be removed.
9. **Built to outlive its founders.** Anyone may copy and continue the
   book under the license. No single person, host or AI provider is
   required for it to survive.
10. **Honest limits.** We never promise perfect safety, guaranteed
    results, universal compatibility or permanence.

## What "decentralized" means here

- The book is plain files anyone can copy, host and verify.
- Every decision is a signed record anyone can re-check (see [TRUST.md](TRUST.md)).
- Other communities can keep their own collections with their own rules.
  Copying our entries does not copy our trust decisions or authority.

## How this charter changes

Through the amendment process in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). The
principles above are the hardest part of the book to change on purpose.
