# Meal Planner: Use What You Already Have

## Purpose

Plan a few days of meals around food you already own, so you buy less
and waste less.

**Who it is for:** anyone cooking on a budget, for themselves or a household.

## What you need

- A list of the food in your kitchen.
- How many meals you need and how many people you are feeding.
- Your spending limit for extra ingredients.
- Dietary restrictions or allergies.
- The cooking equipment and time you have.

Cost: none beyond your groceries. An AI is optional.

## Do it yourself

1. List the food you already have. Put anything close to spoiling at the top.
2. Write down how many meals you need and for how many people.
3. Write down your spending limit, dietary restrictions, and equipment.
4. Pick meals that use the food at the top of your list first.
5. Prefer meals that share ingredients, so one purchase covers two meals.
6. Make a shopping list containing only what is missing.
7. Check quantities and prices before you buy.

## Use with AI

Copy this into any AI chat and fill in the brackets:

```text
Help me plan [NUMBER] meals for [NUMBER] people.

Food I already have (most urgent first):
[LIST]

Budget for extra ingredients:
[AMOUNT]

Dietary restrictions or allergies:
[DETAILS]

Cooking equipment and time available:
[DETAILS]

Suggest a practical meal plan and a shopping list containing only
missing ingredients. Explain substitutions in plain language.
Label prices as estimates unless you verified them.
```

## Check your result

- Every meal uses at least one item you already have.
- The shopping list contains nothing you already own.
- Nothing on the plan conflicts with your restrictions or allergies.
- The estimated total fits your budget.

## Limits and safety

- An AI can make mistakes about allergens. Read ingredient labels yourself.
- Prices from an AI are estimates unless it checked a current source.
- This entry needs no accounts, payments, or personal data. If an AI asks
  for any of those while you use it, stop.

## Required capabilities

None. Works with plain text in any AI chat, or with pencil and paper.
